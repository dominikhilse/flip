Boost inventory

Two icons, a counter, no prose

Two fixed chip slots (one per boost type), always present, dimmed to 0 when empty — and now tinted in the active player's colour like the rest of the chrome. A boost's name shows only as a right-aligned toast that, after a beat, shrinks into its counter and increments it; its explanation appears only in the bottom safe area at the spend-or-stay moment. Max 3 held total (boostMaxHeld).

1-for-2
Solid die + hollow die = keep one, ignore the other. Used when a two-die roll stalls.
Overpay
Double up-chevron = pay past the roll. Spending it XOR-flips the theme as its live signal.

Icon-first everywhere the state is ambient (inventory, toast, dice-used marker). Words appear only where a decision is made — the safe-area prompt and Settings labels — and all of it wears the active player's colour so whose turn it is reads at a glance.
