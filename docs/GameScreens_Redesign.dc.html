How these hold together

One system, the whole flow

Sand = the app talking

Every generic control — primary buttons, selected segments, active tabs, on-toggles — is sand, a warm tone that is not one of the twelve player hues. So no screen can be mistaken for "belonging" to a player who isn't there.

Player hue = identity

A player's colour appears only where that player is the subject: their name, avatar ring, handoff card and results row. Never as chrome.

Red = destructive only

Reserved for End / Restart and the confirm dialog's destructive action. It never doubles as a highlight, so its meaning stays unambiguous.

Flat-to-play

The handoff is driven by laying the phone flat; the "tap to begin" shortcut is a hidden dev affordance, not part of the real loop.

Avatars: all 19 live in app/avatars/ — Mia (05), Sam (03) and Theo (12) shown here. A colour+monogram circle remains the fallback before a player picks one. Benched: per-player end scores on the Results screen await scoring support in the build; the design is final and marked so the code executor leaves it stubbed until then.
